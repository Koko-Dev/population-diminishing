🕯️ A Woman You Will Never Know

She did everything right.

She waited. She prepared. She hoped. She saved.

And she tried — again and again — to welcome life into her arms.

But there were no arms. No cries. No heartbeat. Only silence.

And now the silence echoes.

This is not written for her. She already knows this grief.

This is written for those who may follow her unknowingly — into the same empty waiting room, the same thousand-dollar risk, the same final whisper: "There is nothing more we can do."

We do not speak her name. But we speak her truth.
